    📋 Complete Step-by-Step Test Scenarios

    Test Scenario 1: Artisan Identity Verification & Admin Approval

  • Go to Sign In (/#/login) and click Pending Verification Seller (Siwa Salt).
    • 2. Open Identity Verification Upload tab in the Artisan Portal. 3. Observe store status is PENDING_VERIFICATION and identity document Fatima_Siwa_National_ID.pdf is attached. 4. Sign out and sign in as Platform Administrator (admin@fibyti.eg / Admin123!). 5. In the Admin Dashboard, notice the Verification Queue badge counter (1). 6. Click Inspect ID File (Secure Admin Route) to inspect the protected verification document. 7. Click Approve & Activate Seller Store. 8. Result: The seller's status immediately updates to APPROVED and moves to the active sellers table. ---

    Test Scenario 2: Seller Product Creation & Stock Tracking

  • Sign in as Amina (Approved Seller) (artisan.amina@fibyti.eg / Seller123!).
    • 2. Navigate to Artisan Portal -> My Craft Listings. 3. Click Publish New Handmade Product. 4. Fill out form fields: - Title: Fayoum Terracotta Glazed Jug - Category: Pottery & Ceramics - Price in EGP: 450 - Materials: Natural Nile Clay, Cobalt Glaze - Dimensions: Height 22cm, Diameter 14cm - Weight: 0.8 kg - Lead Time: 2 days - Stock Quantity: 10 5. Click Save Product Listing. 6. Result: The new product is published instantly and appears in the live marketplace catalog. ---

    Test Scenario 3: Multi-Seller Cart & Split Shipping

  • Sign in as Sarah (Buyer) (buyer.sarah@fibyti.eg / Buyer123!).
    • 2. Go to Browse Marketplace (/#/catalog). 3. Add Fayoum Turquoise Glazed Clay Vase (from Amina Fayoum Studio) to cart. 4. Add Akhmim 100% Egyptian Cotton Throw (from Nile Looms) to cart. 5. Open Cart (/#/cart). 6. Notice items are grouped into 2 distinct Artisan Studio Shipments. 7. Select shipping per seller: - Amina Studio: Select Egypt Post (40 EGP). - Nile Looms: Select Private Express Courier (65 EGP) or Direct Pickup (0 EGP). 8. Observe combined shipping total and grand total automatically update. ---

    Test Scenario 4: Checkout, Gateway Fees & 2% Platform Commission

  • Click Proceed to Checkout.
    • 2. Enter delivery address details (Full Name, Phone Number, Governorate, City, Street Address). 3. Select Payment Option: - E-Wallets (Vodafone Cash / InstaPay) - Bank Cards (Visa / Mastercard test card 4242 4242 4242 4242) - Cash on Delivery (COD) 4. Note transparent payment gateway fee (2.5% + 3 EGP for Card/Wallet, 0 EGP for COD). 5. Click Confirm Order. 6. Result: Order is confirmed (#FB-2026-XXXX). The system records the exact 2% Platform Commission on product subtotal and 98% net seller earnings. ---

    Test Scenario 5: Order Status Update & WhatsApp Demo Inspector

  • Sign in as Amina (Approved Seller) -> Orders & Fulfillment.
    • 2. Click Update Fulfillment Status & Trigger WhatsApp. 3. Change status from CONFIRMED to IN_SHIPMENT, enter tracking number EGPOST-98231-FAY, and submit. 4. Observe the green WhatsApp notification box showing the exact Meta WhatsApp Business API JSON payload. 5. Sign in as Platform Administrator -> WhatsApp Demo Inspector tab to view the complete audit log of all simulated messages. ---
